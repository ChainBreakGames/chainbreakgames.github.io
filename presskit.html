About the Studios

ChainBreakGames

Publisher & Co-Developer

ChainBreak Games e.U. is an independent one-person studio based in Lambach, Austria, run by Marc Koppler. The studio focuses on funny multiplayer experiences. Undervalued is ChainBreak's debut title.
